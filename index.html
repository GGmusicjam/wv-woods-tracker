American Ginseng (Panax quinquefolius)

Target Species

Look For: 3-4 prongs radiating from central stalk, each with 5 serrated leaflets. Center cluster turns bright crimson red in late summer.

Maidenhair Fern (Adiantum pedatum)

Prime Indicator

Look For: Delicate fan-shaped fronds on wiry dark brown/black stems. Loves moist, calcium-rich slopes.

Blue Cohosh (Caulophyllum thalictroides)

Prime Indicator

Look For: Powder-blue cast foliage with pea-sized blue seeds in late summer.

Aspect & Microclimates

  • Target Compass Heading: Slopes facing 315° (NW) through 090° (E). True North (000°) and Northeast (045°) are consistently the most productive.
  • Position: Lower-to-middle third hollows and benches with rich, moist leaf mold.
  • Overstory: Sugar Maple, American Basswood, Yellow Buckeye, and Tulip Poplar.

WV Harvest Regulations

  • Season: September 1 to November 30 only.
  • Red Polygons: Harvesting prohibited on WV State Lands, State Forests, WMAs, and Wilderness Areas.
  • National Forests: Harvesting in Monongahela NF requires a district USFS permit.
  • Replanting: Always plant ripe seeds 1" deep within 100 feet of harvest site.
