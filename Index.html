West Virginia Field Guide

🏛️ Legal Public Land Collection:
  • Monongahela National Forest: Harvesting allowed with a valid district USFS permit. Off-limits in Wilderness Areas (Cranberry, Dolly Sods, etc.) and Fernow Experimental Forest.
  • WV State Lands (Parks, Forests, WMAs): STRICTLY PROHIBITED. No digging on any state-managed land.
  • Private Land: Allowed only with written, signed permission from the owner carried with you.
🌿 Habitat & Terrain Indicators:
  • Slope / Aspect: North and East-facing slopes (cool, rich microclimates with 70–80% canopy shade).
  • Companion Plants: Look for Maidenhair Fern, Blue Cohosh, Black Cohosh, Bloodroot, Rattlesnake Fern, and Wild Yam.
  • Overstory Trees: Sugar Maple, American Basswood, Yellow Buckeye, White Ash, and Tulip Poplar.
🌱 Plant Ethics:
  • Must have at least 3 prongs (15 leaflets) and ripe red berries.
  • Always plant ripe seeds 1" deep right at the harvest site.
